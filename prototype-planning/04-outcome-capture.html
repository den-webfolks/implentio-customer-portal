Outcome capture

How does a customer record QuickBox's answer (the amount, the date QuickBox answered, and why) quickly enough that they actually do it, and precisely enough that Credit outcomes (05) and guidance (06) can learn from it?

ExistingNewly confirmedProposedUnresolved· Mock data is labelled; package rows and history are synthetic.

1

Problem

Today the customer records QuickBox's answer on the dispute card, one finding at a time, right in the card. The client likes this, and it stays. The weak part is what gets saved. Right now it's too little and partly wrong, so 05 and 06 would learn the wrong lessons from it.

L4 · The reason for a denial is rarely capturedExisting

Clicking Denied saves the answer immediately. The reason is optional and only asked for afterwards, through an "Add reason" link ("What did the Biller say?"). Once the row says Denied, the job feels done. A partial answer has a reason field in the data, but nothing in the UI fills it.

L3 · The date is when someone logged it, not when QuickBox answeredExisting

One-click answers save today's date, and it can't be edited. n344 asks for a date on partial answers. Any "how fast does QuickBox answer" number would really measure how late the customer logged it:

Sep 8Dispute sent
→
Sep 10QuickBox answers
→
Sep 17Tori logs it

Saved today: date: "2026-09-17", so it reads as "QuickBox took 9 days". The truth is 2 days.

L2 · A dispute QuickBox never answers can't be closedExisting

It stays Waiting on Biller forever, or someone records Denied just to clear it. That false denial has no reason, and 05 would count it and 06 would repeat it. "No reply yet" was removed on 2026-09-25, but it was only a reminder, not a way to close the dispute.

Meeting: capture why a dispute was deniedNewly confirmedCompatible addition

"Free text is enough for now." Denials should become reusable history: what QuickBox said, for which kind of finding, and when. That history stays within this customer's account (n347), and it is the customer's report, not Implentio's judgement (n350).

What stays: the parts the client likedNewly confirmedExisting
  • Answers are recorded finding by finding, right in the dispute card, with no dialog. The columns stay fixed (finding | amount | answer), only one row is open at a time, and Enter saves, Escape cancels.
  • No whole-dispute shortcut like "all collected" (user feedback, 2026-09-24).
  • Change on a recorded answer, with the history of who changed what (n345).
  • The labels Fully collected, Partly collected and Denied, and the card statuses Waiting on Biller, Some outcomes recorded and Done.

Who recorded it, and Implentio-handled disputes

UnresolvedC11 · Potential conflict

Every answer shows who recorded it. When Implentio handles the dispute (03), who records QuickBox's answer conflicts with n344 ("customer-entered, never inferred by Implentio"), n350 (the customer's report, not Implentio's), n347 and D8 ("Recorded by your team"). Q-O4 decides. The two options:

(a) Implentio records it, and the customer can correct it

Data arrives without waiting on the customer. But the answer is Implentio's report, which breaks n344 and n350 as written.

(b) Implentio reports it, and the customer confirms it

Keeps n344 and n350. It adds a step, and unconfirmed answers leave 05 without data.

"Closed, no answer": why it exists and what it must not do

ProposedUnresolved · Q-S3C9 · Potential conflict

Why: without it, the only way to finish a dispute QuickBox ignored is a false Denied. That pollutes the denial reasons in 05, and it makes "Which Billers respond?" (the response rate, Q-D2) impossible to measure.

When: offered only after the dispute has waited N days, shown as after 30 days? Unresolved. Before that, the ⋯ menu shows it greyed out, with the date it becomes available.

How: it's hidden in the row's ⋯ menu, not added as a fourth answer, so it never competes with the three real answers. The note is optional.

Conflict C9: on 2026-09-25 the team removed "No reply yet" and the 7-day reminder ("keep only what the prototype had"). This is different: the customer chooses it, and nothing reminds them. It is still a new state, though, and needs sign-off.

Money and recovery rate: Unresolved. The tracker's rate is recovered ÷ (recovered + not recovered). "No answer" must not quietly count as Not recovered until Q-S3 decides:

The plan's proposed rate C5 (recovered ÷ answered) leaves "no answer" out by definition, because nothing was answered. The mocks below show the amount on its own line, "Closed, no answer $X", as a placeholder.

Why wait (UX reasons):

  • There isn't enough data. The demo account has about 1 recorded denial. Categories would be guessed, not learned.
  • Customers word it differently. Forcing a category too early makes people pick the nearest wrong one, and QuickBox's exact words are lost. Those words are what makes a reason useful in 06.
  • Free text can be grouped later. 05 groups reasons by hand or by keyword. When the same phrasing keeps coming back, suggestions become worth offering, and an optional reasonTag can be added (plan G, 05).
  • Categories shared across customers would be pooled knowledge (C4, n347). They are not part of this.

How it reads Proposed, only if Q-S4 = yes: the finding's result combines its attempts. It stays Partly collected · $20.00 of $35.10, and the line under it says the remaining $15.10 was disputed again and denied. It never shows a red Denied chip when money was recovered.

Why store it per attempt: Q-S4 is open, but the data shouldn't assume "no". If the answer only lived on the finding, the second dispute would overwrite the first, and the $20.00 partial answer and its date would be lost.

          
For 05: attempts count only for history and time to answer: two answers, each with its own date. If Q-S4 says "no", every finding has exactly one attempt, and nothing else changes.
