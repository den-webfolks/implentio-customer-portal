    Recommended order

    Three lenses, then the recommendation that balances them.

      Decisions needed before building

      The plan review flagged the first three as product-logic decisions — they are yours and the client's to make, not the plan's.

      1 · Past the deadline — which option? (C2, Q-S1)A flag only · B separate bucket (memo can be Done) · C ask once · D open, aged. No recommendation; B closes by the calendar one level up. Compare them on a real memo →
      2 · Implentio-managed at what level? (Q-O8)Per dispute, per Biller, or the whole account? Delegating accounts never tick findings, so the level decides the design. See both models →
      3 · Who records answers on Implentio-managed disputes? (C11, Q-O4)n344/n350 say outcomes are the customer's report. (a) Implentio records it and the customer can correct it, or (b) Implentio reports it and the customer confirms it before it counts? Options →
      4 · What couldn't the client find? (Q-E1)Decides where evidence records open, and whether the fix is content or container. Explore →
      5 · Can a finding be disputed again? (Q-S4)After a denial, or for the unpaid part of a partial answer. Either way, store answers per dispute attempt.
      6 · One recovery rate (C5, Q-D1)Recovered ÷ answered (proposed; waiting never counts as failure) or n348's collected ÷ pursued?
