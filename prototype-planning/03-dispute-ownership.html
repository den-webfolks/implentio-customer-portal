Problem

Only the customer can own a dispute today

The only path is customer-managed Existing. Implentio’s part is an opt-in “CC my Implentio support team” checkbox, and by n326 a CC must never read as Implentio owning, sending or following up the dispute.

The meeting asks for an Implentio-managed path

On 2026-09-24 “Ask Implentio to send it” was deferred (D3), and the user asked where the idea came from. The client meeting now asks to support both paths. Confirmed change of direction (C3). Timing and scope are Unresolved. Confirm the meeting’s exact scope before any build: which model, which accounts, which release.

Nobody owns a dispute in the data (L8)

sentBy is a person and via is connected or manual. No field says who owns it. A CSM who already sends for a customer has no way to record that. Some accounts delegate entirely (“Magic Mind delegates entirely”, BI interviews), so for them the per-dispute flow is the wrong shape.

Who records the Biller’s answer is a conflict

n344 says outcomes are “never inferred by Implentio”. n350 says Denied is the customer’s report. n347 labels reasons “Customer-entered”. D8 captions credits “Recorded by your team”. An Implentio-managed dispute breaks all four unless the customer confirms. Potential conflict C11, with C10 Needs verification.

Constraint from the meeting context

Implentio may later use agents to handle disputes. Nothing here designs AI. The model only records who acted (a person on the customer’s team, Implentio staff, or later an Implentio agent). The UI always says “Implentio”, never a named person or bot, so the actor can change without a UX change. Proposed

Proposed model

3.1 The first question: at what level is ownership decided? Unresolved · Q-O8

Two peer models. Neither is recommended until Implentio answers Q-O8 (per dispute, per Biller, or per account) and Q-O1 (who can use it). The rest of this file works for both. Where they differ, it says so.

M1 · Choose per dispute

Proposed option

The customer ticks findings as today, then answers “Who will contact QuickBox?” in step 1 of Dispute with QuickBox.

  • Who selects findings: the customer (D6 unchanged).
  • Deadline pressure: on the customer until they hand over.
  • Fits: accounts that dispute themselves and sometimes want help.
  • Needs: lane B in the dialog and the new card states. No settings.
  • Weak for: delegating accounts. They’d still have to open every memo, tick and hand over.

M2 · Delegate per Biller or account

Proposed option

A setting: “Implentio handles our disputes with QuickBox”. New memos for that Biller are Implentio’s move. The customer does not tick findings. Implentio selects, sends and follows up. There’s a per-memo override: “Dispute this memo yourself instead”.

  • Who selects findings: Implentio. D6 needs re-reading (3.3).
  • Deadline pressure: moves off the customer, who sees the date but no to-do. Whether Implentio commits to sending before it is Q-O3.
  • Fits: accounts that delegate entirely and rarely open the app.
  • Needs: a setting, “whose move” wording across the tracker, and rules for leave-outs and Won’t pursue.
  • Weak for: customers who want to review each claim before it goes.
Q-O8 decides this. Is the choice made per dispute, per Biller, or per account?Both can coexist: M2 for delegating accounts, M1 for everyone else, with M1’s step-1 choice hidden or preset on delegated Billers. That combination costs the most to build. Ask first whether any account besides the delegating ones would use Implentio handling per dispute.

Risk: M2 can leave dead ends for accounts that rarely log in

Unresolved · for the user to decide

Each piece looks reasonable alone. Together they give a delegated memo that can’t close without the customer, which is exactly who M2 is for.

M2 · Who may mark Won’t pursue? Unresolved · Q-03a

  • (i) Only the customer. Findings Implentio leaves out come back as Ready to dispute with Implentio’s reason: “Implentio left this out: too small to dispute on its own.” It’s the customer’s move to dispute it or mark Won’t pursue. This keeps Won’t pursue a customer decision (n359, n360).
  • (ii) Implentio may mark it for the customer. It shows as “Won’t pursue · decided by Implentio” with the reason and an Undo. Fewer loose ends for accounts that never log in, but it changes what Won’t pursue means.

M2 · How D6 (no pre-selection) applies Unresolved · Q-03a

D6 stops the app from ticking findings for the customer. Under M2 the customer has delegated the choice, so Implentio’s selection is a decision made for them, not a pre-selection. Proposed reading: the app still never pre-ticks anything. Implentio’s choice is labelled as Implentio’s, and every left-out finding shows why. D6 was decided for customer-managed disputes, so this needs the user’s confirmation.

3.4 Who records the Biller’s answer? Potential conflict · C11 Unresolved · Q-O4

This isn’t a given. n344: outcomes are “recorded by the customer … never inferred by Implentio”. n350: Denied is the customer’s report, never Implentio’s determination. n347: reasons are shown as “Customer-entered”. D8: credits are “Recorded by your team”. And C10: nobody confirms that the credit actually arrived.

(a) Implentio records it

  • Breaks n344, n350 and n347 as written, and D8’s “Recorded by your team” caption.
  • “Not right?” lets the customer correct it, with history (n345).

(b) Implentio reports it, you confirm

Option

  • Keeps n344, n350 and D8. Until confirmed, the money isn’t counted as Recovered. The card says “Answer to confirm”, not “Waiting on Biller”, because QuickBox has answered.

  • The tracker flags “Answer to confirm” as the customer’s move.
Either way
