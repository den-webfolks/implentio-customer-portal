QuickBox remote-area shows 0% because it mixes a $1,200 denial with $2,700 that is still waiting. The tracker's "Recovery rate" uses a different definition. Potential conflict · C5

The learning part is buried and technical

"Biller credit outcomes" sits at the bottom, under a 12-column ledger. It uses category names like "DAS Classification Discrepancy" and "Packages with base freight as the primary variance driver". The Biller's reason is hidden behind "View reason" in one expandable row. A trainee can't tell what worked.

The data can't answer "how fast" or "do they answer"

An answer's date is the day someone recorded it (L3). No result closes a dispute the Biller never answers (L2). Reasons are optional and asked for only after saving (L4). Any speed or response metric today would measure how late your team logged it, not the Biller.

Metric definitions

Proposed Needs sign-off · C5 · Q-D1

The page's "How we count" link shows the plain version of these. They are shared with 06 (guidance), which reads the same numbers.

Recovered (recovery rate)

recovered $ ÷ $ disputed and answered

Answered = the Biller said fully collected, partly collected or denied. Waiting is left out and shown apart. Closed, no answer is its own grey segment and is not counted as not recovered until Q-S3 decides.

Always shown with its counts: "3 of 7 paid · 19% of the money". The rate is by money; 06 speaks in counts. Each finding's amount counts once, however many times it was disputed.

Today (n348): collected ÷ pursued, which includes waiting. Potential conflict · C5

Worked example: ShipBob's 0%

n348 today: ShipBob shipping price = $0 ÷ $3,410.11 = 0%. The dispute was sent Sep 12 and hasn't been answered.

Proposed: nothing answered → "No answers yet · 1 waiting". No rate, never 0%.

QuickBox remote-area: n348 = $0 ÷ $3,900 = 0%. Proposed = $0 ÷ $1,200 answered, but that's 1 answer → "Not enough answers yet (1)", with $2,700 shown as waiting.

QuickBox overall: $11,700 ÷ $14,500 answered = 81% of the money. But with 3 answers the page shows "2 of 3 paid · $11,700.00 of $14,500.00". Percentages start at 6 answers.

What counts as one "dispute" Proposed

One finding sent to a Biller. One email to QuickBox with 3 findings counts as 3. Money is counted once per finding.

If a finding can be disputed again (only if Q-S4 = yes): its result combines its attempts. Example: the fuel finding ($35.10) got $20.00 on the first attempt; the rest was disputed again and denied. The finding shows Partly collected, $20.00 of $35.10, "the rest was disputed again and denied". Its $35.10 enters the recovery rate's base once.

Attempts still count for history (Recent answers, the Biller timeline) and for time to answer, one measurement per attempt. A changed answer replaces the earlier one; the earlier one stays in the history (n345).

The customer-facing word for this is Q-05a.

Time to answer Not measured yet

median(Biller's answer date − sent date)

Needs 04's answeredOn. Today only the recording date exists (L3), so the real-data view says "Not measured yet" and, at most, "recorded 13 days after sending". The mock view assumes 04 captures the answer date.

Answered % Not in v1 · Q-D2

Left out of the page. "Closed, no answer" is tucked away in 04's ⋯ menu, so a no-answer rate would measure whether your team closes stale disputes, not how often a Biller answers.

If Q-D2 wants it: count disputes waiting longer than N days as unanswered for this metric only, without changing any status.

Sample-size tiers (one rule, shared with 06)

  • None, under 3 answers: "Not enough answers yet (2)". No rate.
  • Early signal, 3–5: counts and money, no percentage.
  • Mixed, 6+: under 75% got the same answer. Percentage of money plus counts.
  • Pattern, 6+: at least 75% got the same answer (fully collected, partly collected or denied). Only this tier appears in By Biller's "Patterns" column, as counts with the months covered: "Fuel charge: 6 of 7 paid in full · Sep 2025–Sep 2026".

Counted over the selected period: last 12 months by default, the same window as 06.

Thresholds are Q-G1. The pattern column is Q-05g. One function in this file (tierOf) drives the marker, the rate display and the patterns.

Why no tilesThe money lives on the tracker. The scope line says how much history the page is built on, which tells the reader how far to trust it. Proposed
Why counts in the barThe mix bar is by count, so each dispute weighs the same. The recovery rate is by money. Both appear side by side, so one large denial can't hide (see edge cases). Weighting is Q-05b. Proposed
ReasonsQuotes are shown word for word, labelled by who recorded them: "Customer-entered" (n347), or "QuickBox's reason, recorded by Implentio" when Implentio handled the dispute (C11). Group names describe what the text mentions ("Mentions a contract addendum"). They never state the Biller's reason as a fact (n350). Existing rule

    Open questions

    Q-D1 · Recovery rate definitionRecovered ÷ disputed-and-answered (proposed, C5) or n348's collected ÷ pursued? The proposal changes n348 and the tracker's rate must match whichever wins.
    Q-D2 · Response metricsTime to answer needs the Biller's answer date (04); until then it says "Not measured yet". Answered % is left out of v1: "Closed, no answer" sits in a ⋯ menu, so it would measure your team's tidiness. If wanted, count disputes waiting longer than N days as unanswered for this metric only, without changing any status. Is measuring responsiveness wanted?
    Q-D3a · Pooled denial reasonsForbidden by n347. Confirm that still holds, including anonymised themes.
    Q-D3b · Pooled outcome ratesAre anonymised, Implentio-wide outcome rates per Biller allowed as a separate, labelled source? Not covered by n347.
    Q-D4 · How much history is typical?How many answered disputes does a typical account have per Biller × type per year? If it's a handful, most rows will say "Not enough answers yet" for months, and By Biller may matter more than By variance type.
    Q-D5 · How are findings typed for history?What happens when the title and the charge mix disagree? Examples: "Shipping price and fuel" (154 packages); a "Home-delivery fee" finding whose home-delivery charges were $48.36 below contract; $1,322.77 of "Shipping price" that is really fuel. Options: by title (this mock), split by charge, or the larger charge. Linked to Q-E7 (02).
    Q-T1 · NamingThis page says "variance type" for what the app calls a variance group (D7, still open). Also: collected vs recovered ("Recovered" here is the money word), Closed, no answer, Handled by Implentio, and the proposed plain name Oversize fee.
    Q-S3 · "Closed, no answer" in the rateFrom 01. This page keeps it out of the recovery rate and shows it apart. After how many days can it be chosen?
    Q-G1 · Sample thresholdsShared with 06: under 3 = no rate, 3–5 = counts only, 6+ = percentage. Same thresholds on this page as in guidance?
    Q-05a · The word "dispute"The page counts findings sent to a Biller, each finding once. A customer thinks of one email as one dispute. Is "42 disputes answered" right when one email carried 4 findings? Alternatives: "42 findings answered", "42 answers".
    Q-05b · Count or money?The outcome mix is by count, the rate by money, and both are shown. Should one lead? One $18,400 denial can pull a money rate far from the count (Flowspace example).
    Q-05c · Default periodProposed: last 12 months, the same window as 06, so the page and the guidance agree. Should it be "All time" while history is thin? Should the window use the answer date (as in this mock) or the sent date?
    Q-05d · How are denial reasons grouped?Keyword matching (this mock), a tag the customer picks when recording (04), or Implentio staff tagging? Under n350, group names must describe the customer's text, not state the Biller's reason. Who writes them?
    Q-05e · Whole-memo disputesA "Complete credit memo" row in By variance type, or left out of that view and counted elsewhere?
    Q-05f · Changed answers and timeWhen an answer is changed (Partly → Fully), which date counts for time to answer: the first answer or the latest?
    Q-05g · A "Patterns" column in By Biller?Proposed: only Pattern-tier types, as counts with the months covered ("Fuel charge: 6 of 7 paid in full · Sep 2025–Sep 2026"), otherwise —. With account-only history most Billers will show —. Is the column worth it, or should patterns appear only inside detail panels?
    Q-05h · Implentio-recorded answers in the same numbersIf Implentio records answers (C11, Q-O4), do they count in the same rates as your team's answers, or only once the customer confirms them?
